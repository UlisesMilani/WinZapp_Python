File > WhatsApp settings has two tabs, Privacy and Profile, and changes your WhatsApp account directly on WhatsApp.

Privacy tab

  • Last seen and online; About; profile picture; read receipts; who can add you to groups; who can see your status (my contacts, my contacts except..., or share only with...).
  • The people for status are chosen from the synced contacts, in a list with search where each one is marked with Space.
  • The window loads the current values, sends only what you changed and then checks, saying which settings WhatsApp did not confirm.

Profile tab alpha 4397

Changes the display name, the About text and the profile picture of your WhatsApp account without picking up your phone.

  • The tab opens already showing your current name and About text, and whether you have a picture. A field left blank or unchanged stays as it is on WhatsApp.
  • The window opens from anywhere with Ctrl+Shift+, (the Settings key plus Shift); you can change it on the Keyboard shortcuts tab.
  • Each item is saved separately: if WhatsApp refuses one, the others are saved and the window lists the ones that failed.
  • The picture is chosen from a JPG or PNG file.
